2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Office Document: Word, PDF, XLSX is built on an on-device utility architecture. We respect the confidentiality of your documents and personal life:

  • No Document Content Snooping: We do NOT upload, read, collect, monitor, or store the contents of your documents, spreadsheets, presentations, or text files (including DOCX, DOC, XLSX, XLS, PPTX, PPT, PDF, TXT, HWP, EPUB) on any remote server. All file opening, parsing, reading, rendering, and offline editing are performed strictly locally on your device.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information: We do NOT collect personal identity records such as your full legal name, national identity number, passport number, personal telephone number, contacts list, or physical home address.
  • No Private Media Inspection: We do NOT inspect, duplicate, or transmit your private photos, home videos, audio recordings, or non-document files to external cloud servers.
  • No Financial or Credit Card Data: We do NOT collect or store credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses. All in-app subscription purchases are processed directly and securely by Google Play In-App Billing.
  • No GPS Location Tracking: We do NOT request or track your device's precise GPS physical location.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose of Use

To provide essential document reading, document scanning, PDF conversion, and file management functions, the App requests specific device permissions:

3.1 Camera & Scanning

  • android.permission.CAMERA
    Purpose: Used specifically for the Document Scanner (OCR) feature. It allows you to take pictures of physical paper documents, receipts, invoices, book pages, or business cards to convert them into digital PDF files or extract editable text using Optical Character Recognition (OCR).
    Data Protection: Camera capture operates on-demand only when you open the Scanner. Image data is processed locally on your device and is never sent to remote servers without your explicit action.

3.2 Storage & Media Access

  • android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13+ / API Level 33+)
    Purpose: Allows you to select photos or scans from your gallery when using the Image to PDF conversion feature or OCR extraction.
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE & android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below / API Level ≤ 32)
    Purpose: Required on older Android operating systems to discover, open, display, edit, and save document files stored in your device storage or SD card.
  • android.permission.MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (All Files Access - where applicable)
    Purpose: Enables the all-in-one document viewer and manager to index, categorize, browse, rename, delete, and organize all document formats (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, HWP, TXT) across device directories as requested by the user.
    Data Protection: Storage access is used exclusively to facilitate file viewing and document management. No personal files are transferred outside your device.

3.3 Network & Connectivity

  • android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE
    Purpose: Used to verify active network connections for loading non-intrusive advertisements (Google AdMob), validating and restoring VIP subscriptions via Google Play Billing, logging anonymous diagnostics/crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics), and connecting to secure cloud conversion engines when initiated by the user.

3.4 Google Play In-App Billing

  • com.android.vending.BILLING
    Purpose: Enables in-app purchases and VIP subscriptions (ad removal, unlimited conversions) managed securely through Google Play In-App Billing (Play Billing Library 8.0.0). All financial transactions are handled exclusively by Google Play; no payment details are accessible to the developer.

3.5 Advertising Identifier

  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID
    Purpose: Used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide contextual advertising, prevent ad fraud, and adhere to user-selected personalized ad preferences.

3.6 Foreground Service

  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE
    Purpose: Ensures that long-running operations—such as converting or merging multi-page PDF documents, compressing large files, or transferring files over secure local Wi-Fi—can complete successfully in the background without being prematurely terminated by Android's battery saver.

3.7 Notifications

  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+ / API Level 33+)
    Purpose: Sends local notifications when long-running document conversions (e.g. PDF to Word, large file merge) are completed, or when essential app updates/reminders are available. Users can disable notifications at any time in system settings.

3.8 User Experience & Convenience

  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Keeps the screen awake while reading documents or eBooks in Reading Mode so the screen doesn't turn off unexpectedly.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides haptic feedback during interactive controls (page turning, button clicks).
  • com.android.launcher.permission.INSTALL_SHORTCUT: Allows users to create one-tap shortcuts to their favorite or frequently read documents directly on the Android home screen.
